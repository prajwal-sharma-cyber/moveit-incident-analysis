# Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exploitation of vulnerable file-transfer software | High | High | Critical |
| Sensitive data exposure | High | High | Critical |
| Delayed patching | Medium | High | High |
| Insufficient asset visibility | Medium | High | High |
| Weak third-party risk monitoring | Medium | High | High |

## Risk Treatment

### Reduce

Maintain an accurate asset inventory and vulnerability-management process.

### Avoid

Remove unnecessary internet exposure where business requirements allow.

### Transfer

Use contractual and insurance mechanisms where appropriate for third-party risk.

### Accept

Only accept residual risk after documented assessment and approval.
